About McLeod Drilling

35 years. Built on word of mouth.

McLeod Drilling started out running big drilling rigs. Over the years we found a niche: our own drill rigs, built on 6-wheel-drive LandCruisers.

They do the job without tracks and with a small footprint on the ground. That is why government agencies, traditional owners and landowners are happy to see us arrive, and why our work comes from people recommending us.

Home base is now a big commercial shed in Port Augusta.

What we do

Exploration drilling, with a small footprint.

Four drilling methods, all run from light, low-impact rigs, plus the support to keep a crew working out bush.

Our speciality

Aircore drilling

Small-footprint aircore drilling for mineral exploration. Air carries the rock chips up the inside of the rods, so each sample comes up on its own.

Slimline RC drilling

Reverse circulation (RC) drilling on a slimline, low-impact rig, for sampling deeper ground without bringing in heavy gear.

RAB drilling

Rotary air blast (RAB) drilling: a quick way to test shallow ground across a wide area.

Auger drilling and sampling

Auger drilling for soil and calcrete sampling, from a LandCruiser-mounted auger rig.

Remote support and camps

Support vehicles and remote caravan camps available, so the crew is set up wherever the job is.
